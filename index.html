BeReal.

Founding Growth Team · 2022–2024

I joined BeReal in 2022 on the founding growth team, before the app had really taken off outside France. I led the UK launch, growing DAUs 19x in two months and taking BeReal to #1 on the App Store.

From there I moved to international expansion, building and managing on-the-ground teams across the UK, Australia, Indonesia, and the US, and working closely with the COO.

I left in 2024, shortly after BeReal was acquired by Voodoo for €500M.

Retro

Head of Growth · 2024–2025

In 2023 I cold emailed Nathan, Retro's founder, to tell him I loved the product and saw the same early potential I'd seen at BeReal. Six months later I joined as Head of Growth to take it worldwide.

I built and led on-the-ground teams across Europe, Asia, and the US, taking Retro to Top Charts in all three regions. The wildest chapter was Taiwan, where Retro went properly viral.

By the time I left, Retro had gone from a small user base to 7M+ users and was named a 2025 App Store Awards finalist.

Corner

Advisor · 2025

Corner has been one of my favorite consumer products of the last couple of years, so I reached out to Eliza, the founder, and offered to help.

Through 2025 I advised the team on user acquisition and the product features behind growth and retention, mainly onboarding, notifications, and referrals.

GOLD

Product & Growth · 2025–Present

I joined Gold in 2025 to lead product and growth, and took the app from pre-launch to Top Charts in the US. Day to day I work across engineering, design, and ML, deciding what we build and shipping it.

The feature I'm proudest of is our newest search, the one playing on the phone. You describe what you're looking for in plain language, and it recommends pieces based on your taste graph. I led the ML engineers and designers who built it, from first prototype to rollout.
